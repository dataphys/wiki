This bibliography is a **collection of academic work on data
physicalization**. [You can help us extend it](Contribute).

For work that does not refer to data physicalization but describes
technologies which may be used for that purpose, see
[technologies](technologies). For non-academic work, see the [list of
physical visualizations](http://dataphys.org/list).

***Please do not link to articles behind paywalls.*** If you authored an
article for which no link is available, please put your PDF online and
send us the link. The principle of
[self-archiving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-archiving) generally
allows you to do so.
