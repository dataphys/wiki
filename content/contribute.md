If you have been in one way or another involved in studying or
practicing data physicalization, you can ask to be listed on the [people page](people) and also become a contributor to this
wiki. To be added to the people page, please [fill this form](http://goo.gl/forms/S8Q2N8o1ip). If you want to contribute to this site, have a look at the [guide for contributors](guide).

Thank you!
